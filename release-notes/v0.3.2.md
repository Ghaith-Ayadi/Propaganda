# v0.3.2

October 20, 2026 · The Strategist starts from your readers' searches

- **Searches first.** The plan now starts from the Google searches the people you sell to make about their own work, priced wide: volume, difficulty, how many sites link to page one, the six-month trend, the peak month, and whether Google answers with an AI overview. A search nobody has a number for says so, instead of counting as zero.
- **Three kinds of target.** 15 to 20 searches in three buckets: winnable this quarter, must win over time (the big searches, with a position goal), and no data yet. Your own name and the pages you host for customers are never targets.
- **Your profile.** The plan opens with who you are according to what you gave us, and says who ranks for your readers' searches today.
- **Bigger plans.** The volume is the number you said you can review, and the plan carries up to eight topics. Your product belongs in a topic when its design answers the reader's problem.
- **Goals page.** The searches table shows the buckets in colour, the new columns, and what each column means on hover; topic cards show the thesis, with the reasoning behind "Read more".
