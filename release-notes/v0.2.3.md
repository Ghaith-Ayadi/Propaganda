# v0.2.3

October 14, 2026 · Strategist inputs and house style

- **Strategist.** It reads a tenant's website without the illustrations, demo screens and hidden text, keeps the headings, and follows the site's own menu, so a plan cites what the tenant says.
- **Topics.** Each topic has a short name and a one-sentence thesis. Slogans from the tenant's site no longer become topics, and keyword research starts from words that fit the tenant.
- **House style.** The Strategist, the Pitcher, the Writer and Chat follow twenty writing rules. Drafts and briefs come out with no em dashes and no filler phrases.
- **Release notes.** Every release has its notes in the app at /release-notes, and the command palette shows the current version.
- **Admin.** The Arena offers Qwen3.7 Plus as a default rival and suggests model names as you type.
