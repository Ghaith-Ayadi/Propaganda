# v0.4.0

October 22, 2026 · The Claude plugin, real blog pages and a team

- **Claude plugin.** Install it in Claude desktop and ask for a post to be written for one of your collections. You can list your collections and assign a post to one, with your own notes and source material attached, and it goes through the same drafting, review and fact check as any other post. Access is by a token you create in Settings.
- **Collection rules.** Each collection can carry rules for the people and agents who write into it.
- **Your style, in the knowledge base.** The knowledge base now holds Styles next to Facts: your own rules for how things are written, read by every agent that writes for you.
- **People.** Invite teammates by email, give them a role, and see their names where they edited.
- **Blog pages for search engines and language models.** Every page of your blog is now real HTML on the server, with its address, preview image and title in the page, a sitemap, an RSS feed and a robots file. Sites also publish llms.txt, a Markdown copy of every post, structured data with the author and breadcrumbs, and tell search engines when a page changes. A collection that does not exist now answers 404.
- **Strategy and chat.** The plan reads your own blog before it proposes anything, and chat can read the plan and your website. The pitches keep each claim's real source, and the review panel's source check now covers every factual claim.
- **Settings.** One Site section, a themed blog for new tenants and a domain step in Getting started. Only the settings a public blog needs can be read without signing in.
- **Smaller.** Slow model calls no longer time out, titles are one line, we say "we" instead of naming agents, the installed app's icon refreshes, and a dropped connection is reported only after five minutes.
- **Safer reading.** The pages we fetch for you are read through a guarded connection.

The schema gains tables for collection rules, access tokens, styles and invites, and tightens who can read a site's settings. No data is changed or removed.
