# v0.4.2

October 22, 2026 · Connect Claude by signing in

- **No token to paste.** In Claude, add a custom connector with the address shown in Settings > Connections > Claude, or press Connect on the Propaganda plugin. Claude opens Propaganda, you pick the blog and press Allow, and it appears in your list of connections.
- **Tokens still work.** For scripts and other tools that cannot sign in, you can still make a token in the same place.

No data is changed or removed, and there are no schema changes.
