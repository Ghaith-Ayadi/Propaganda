# v0.2.6

October 14, 2026 · Strategist, pitches, Chat and Arena

- **Strategist.** The plan answers the buyer's question and no longer shuns pages that rank for it.
- **Pitches.** Each pitch leans on the tenant's own subject instead of arguing against the pages that rank.
- **One idea, one budget.** A single example can no longer run through every topic, pitch and draft.
- **Chat.** It reads your goals, searches pitches and ideas, and finds a sentence you paste.
- **Arena.** It runs writing rounds on tenants by their slug, and its Chat replay shows how Chat answers.
- **Writer.** Its prompt moved into its own file so the Arena and the Writer use the same one. Drafts do not change.
